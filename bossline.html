Toy March: Tin Army Battles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Toy March: Tin Army Battles ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game and its partners process. In short: we collect nothing ourselves and run no servers; the ads in the game come from Google AdMob, which processes device data as described below.

No account, no data collected by us

The game has no account or sign-in. We do not collect, sell or receive any personal data, and the game sends nothing to us. It asks for no permission to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (toy coins, rank, upgrades, unlocked maps, best times and records), the daily toy chest timer, ad-pacing counters (for example when the last full-screen ad was shown) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics quality) are stored in the game's private storage on your device. This data does not leave your device, except that Android's own device backup may include it if you have backup turned on in Android Settings; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus, and an occasional full-screen ad between battles. There are no banner ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options (ads). If you do not consent, ads are not personalised.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • When no ad is available, the game may show its own full-screen card for another Ixvil Group game, marked "Ad". To keep that list current, the game downloads a small public list of our games from our website (ixvil.github.io, hosted by GitHub); the request carries no personal data, though, like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the host. The game checks on your device which of our games are already installed, to show "Open" instead of "Get"; this information never leaves your device.

Map downloads (Google Play)

The first map is part of the install. The other four maps are downloaded through Google Play (Play Asset Delivery) right after installation, the same way Google Play downloads app updates. Google Play handles this download under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

No in-app purchases

The game sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases. The Settings screen offers a "Restore purchases" button from the shared game framework; it only asks Google Play whether your account owns earlier purchases and is not needed for this game.

No analytics or tracking by us

The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK of its own. Gameplay events (such as a battle starting or ending) are written only to the device's local system log for debugging and are not sent anywhere. The game does not use Google Play Games sign-in.

No notifications

The game does not send push or local notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

You can withdraw or change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (ads), reset the advertising ID in Android Settings, and delete all of the game's data at any time by deleting the app (or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps).

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
