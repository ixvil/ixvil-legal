Bubble Bloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Bubble Bloom ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game and its partners process.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, avatar, progress, scores, coins, purchases status and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers and do not collect this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads; you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options (ads).
  • On iOS the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your device identifier.
  • Buying "Remove ads" disables interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available at your choice.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card data. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores, achievements and Game Center nickname are processed by Apple to show leaderboards and friends.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device. No data leaves the device for this.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

You can reset progress in Profile → Reset progress, withdraw ad consent in Settings, disable tracking and notifications in iOS Settings, and delete all local data by deleting the app.
