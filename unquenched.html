Unquenched: Eater of Souls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Unquenched: Eater of Souls ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game processes. In short: the game collects nothing and shares nothing; the one in-app purchase is processed by Google Play.

No data collected or shared

The game does not collect, sell or share any personal or device data, and it sends nothing to us. It has no account or sign-in, and it asks for no permissions to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Network access

The game works offline. It does not contact our servers (we do not operate any). The only connection it makes is to Google Play, through the Google Play Billing Library, to show the price of the in-app purchase, to make or restore that purchase, and to check whether your Google account owns it. That is why the game's Android manifest lists internet and network-state permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (your hero, items, story choices and the chosen difficulty), your settings (graphics, sound and voice volume) and whether the full game is unlocked on this device are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Android's cloud backup is switched off for the game, so the save is not copied to Google's backup either.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier.

In-app purchase

The Prologue and Act I are free. One optional in-app purchase, "The Whole Road", unlocks Act II, Act III, the Finale and every ending. There are no other purchases, no ads and nothing that buys power. Purchases are processed by Google Play: you pay in Google Play's own purchase screen, under Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never see your card or payment details. The game only receives from Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, keeps that answer on your device so the unlock also works offline, and checks it again when Google Play is reachable; a refunded purchase locks the later acts again but never deletes your progress. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

No notifications

The game does not send push or local notifications.

Google Play

Downloads, updates and the in-app purchase are handled by Google Play. Google may process data about your installation and purchases (for example your purchase history, and diagnostic data from the Google Play Billing Library) under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We receive no personal data from this.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children: it contains fantasy violence, fire and dark themes. Because the game collects no data at all, it does not collect personal data from children either.

Your choices

You can delete all the game's data at any time by deleting the app (or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps). Starting a new game over an existing save replaces the save.

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
