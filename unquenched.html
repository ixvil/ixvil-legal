Unquenched: Eater of Souls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Unquenched: Eater of Souls ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game and its partners process. In short: we collect nothing ourselves and run no servers; the optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob, and optional purchases are handled by Google Play.

No account, no data collected by us

The game has no account or sign-in. We do not collect, sell or receive any personal data, and the game sends nothing to us. It asks for no permission to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (your hero, items, story choices, the chosen trial), your looks and the state of the Reckoning, counters for the optional ads (how many you watched today) and your settings (graphics, sound and voice volume) are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. Android's cloud backup is switched off for the game, so the save is not copied to Google's backup either.

The whole game is free

The Prologue, every act, the Finale and every ending are free. Gear, levels, souls and power are earned by playing.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

At the village you may choose to watch a short video ad for a small in-game bonus. Ads are opt-in only: never shown during a fight, never forced, with a daily cap. There are no banner or interstitial ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Optional purchases (Google Play)

The game may offer optional cosmetic looks and an optional premium track of the Reckoning, bought through Google Play Billing. You pay in Google Play's own purchase screen, under Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives from Google Play whether your account owns an item and keeps that answer on your device. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Downloads (Google Play)

Downloads, updates and the game's large content packs (Play Asset Delivery) are handled by Google Play, which may process data about your installation under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We receive no personal data from this.

No analytics or tracking by us

The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK of its own and does not use Google Play Games sign-in.

No notifications

The game does not send push or local notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

Children

The game contains fantasy violence and dark themes, is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

You can simply not watch ads, change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options, reset the advertising ID in Android Settings, and delete all of the game's data at any time by deleting the app (or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps).

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
