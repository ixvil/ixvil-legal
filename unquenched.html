Unquenched: Eater of Souls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Unquenched: Eater of Souls ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game processes. In short: the game collects nothing and shares nothing.

No data collected or shared

The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. It has no account or sign-in, and it asks for no permissions to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

No network access

The game does not request internet access and works fully offline. It does not contact our servers (we do not operate any) or any third-party service while you play.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (your hero, items, story choices and the chosen difficulty) and your settings (graphics, sound and voice volume) are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Android's cloud backup is switched off for the game, so the save is not copied to Google's backup either.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier.

No in-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases and no payment features.

No notifications

The game does not send push or local notifications.

Google Play

Downloads and updates are handled by Google Play. Google may process data about your installation under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We receive no personal data from this.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children: it contains fantasy violence, fire and dark themes. Because the game collects no data at all, it does not collect personal data from children either.

Your choices

You can delete all the game's data at any time by deleting the app (or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps). Starting a new game over an existing save replaces the save.

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
