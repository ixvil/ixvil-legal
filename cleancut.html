Clean Cut: Samurai Duel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Clean Cut: Samurai Duel ("the game") is published by Alexey Shipin. This policy explains what data the game and its partners process. In short: we collect nothing ourselves and run no servers; the ads in the game come from Google AdMob, which processes device data as described below.

No account, no data collected by us

The game has no account or sign-in. We do not collect, sell or receive any personal data, and the game sends nothing to us. It asks for no permission to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Besides internet access for ads, the only permission it uses is vibration, for the haptic feedback of a cut.

Data stored on your device

Your progress on the road (stars per stage, opened provinces, trial scores, mon, owned and equipped cosmetics) and ad-pacing counters (how many runs since the last full-screen ad) are stored in the game's private storage on your device. This data does not leave your device, except that Android's own device backup may include it if you have backup turned on in Android Settings; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows an occasional full-screen ad served by Google AdMob between runs (about once every three runs, never during a duel). You may also choose to watch an optional rewarded ad ("Sharpen the blade") to make the sword glint brighter for your next three tries; it is only shown when you tap the button. There are no banner ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Privacy options (ads). If you do not consent, ads are not personalised.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • When no ad is available, the game may show its own full-screen card for another Ixvil Group game, marked "Ad". To keep that list current, the game may download a small public list of our games from our website (ixvil.github.io, hosted by GitHub); the request carries no personal data, though, like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the host. The game checks on your device which of our games are already installed, to show "Open" instead of "Get"; this information never leaves your device.

No in-app purchases

The game sells nothing. Mon (the in-game currency) and all cosmetics are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.

No analytics or tracking by us

The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK of its own. Technical figures (such as the frame rate) are written only to the device's local system log for debugging and are not sent anywhere.

No notifications

The game does not send push or local notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

Children

The game shows stylized violence with blood and is intended for adults (18 and older). It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your choices

You can withdraw or change your ad consent in the game's Privacy options (ads), reset the advertising ID in Android Settings, and delete all of the game's data at any time by deleting the app (or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps).

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
